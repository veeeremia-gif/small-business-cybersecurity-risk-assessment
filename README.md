# Small Business Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

## Overview

This project presents a practical cybersecurity risk assessment for a fictional small business.

The objective was to identify important information and technology assets, assess cybersecurity threats and risks, and develop prioritized security recommendations.

> **Disclaimer:** NordSecure Consulting GmbH is a fictional company created for educational and portfolio purposes.

## Scenario

**Company:** NordSecure Consulting GmbH
**Industry:** Business Consulting
**Employees:** 25
**Location:** Hamburg, Germany

The organization relies on cloud services, company laptops, business email, customer databases and digital document management.

## Methodology

The assessment follows a structured risk management approach based on the:

* NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0
* CIA Triad
* Likelihood × Impact risk scoring

## Project Components

### 1. Company Profile

Defines the organization's business environment, IT environment, information assets and assessment objectives.

### 2. Asset Inventory

Identifies critical technology, information and infrastructure assets.

### 3. Threat Assessment

Identifies potential cybersecurity threats and their potential impact.

### 4. Risk Register

Evaluates risks based on likelihood and impact and assigns risk levels.

### 5. Security Controls

Defines appropriate technical and organizational security controls.

### 6. NIST CSF Mapping

Maps the assessment activities and controls to the NIST CSF 2.0 functions.

### 7. Security Recommendations

Provides prioritized security improvements and a 90-day implementation roadmap.

### 8. Executive Summary

Provides a management-level overview of the identified risks and recommended actions.
