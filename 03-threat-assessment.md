# Threat Assessment

## Objective

The objective of this threat assessment is to identify potential cybersecurity threats affecting NordSecure Consulting GmbH and evaluate their potential impact on the organization's assets.

## Threat Categories

### 1. Phishing

Phishing attacks may target employees in order to obtain credentials or deliver malicious links and attachments.

**Potential targets:**

* Employee accounts
* Microsoft 365
* Business email

**Potential impact:**

* Account compromise
* Unauthorized access
* Data exposure

### 2. Ransomware

Ransomware could affect company laptops, servers or cloud-connected resources.

**Potential targets:**

* Employee laptops
* Cloud storage
* Business data

**Potential impact:**

* Business interruption
* Data loss
* Financial damage

### 3. Credential Theft

Attackers may obtain employee credentials through phishing, password reuse or other techniques.

**Potential targets:**

* Employee accounts
* Microsoft 365
* Business email

**Potential impact:**

* Unauthorized access
* Data theft
* Account takeover

### 4. Unauthorized Access

Weak access controls or compromised credentials could allow unauthorized users to access company resources.

**Potential targets:**

* Customer database
* Cloud storage
* HR records
* Financial records

**Potential impact:**

* Confidentiality breach
* Data manipulation
* Privacy risks

### 5. Cloud Misconfiguration

Incorrect cloud permissions or configurations could expose sensitive company information.

**Potential targets:**

* Cloud storage
* Microsoft 365
* Customer documents

**Potential impact:**

* Unauthorized data access
* Data exposure
* Compliance risks

### 6. Lost or Stolen Device

A lost or stolen company laptop could expose locally stored business information.

**Potential targets:**

* Employee laptops

**Potential impact:**

* Data exposure
* Unauthorized access
* Business disruption

### 7. Insider Threat

A malicious or compromised employee account could be used to access or modify company information.

**Potential targets:**

* Customer database
* Financial records
* HR records
* Cloud storage

**Potential impact:**

* Data loss
* Data manipulation
* Confidentiality breach

### 8. Website Compromise

An attacker could exploit vulnerabilities in the public-facing website.

**Potential targets:**

* Company website

**Potential impact:**

* Website defacement
* Service disruption
* Reputation damage
