# NIST CSF 2.0 Mapping

## GOVERN

Relevant activities:

* Cybersecurity governance
* Risk management
* Security policies
* Roles and responsibilities

## IDENTIFY

Relevant activities:

* Asset inventory
* Business environment analysis
* Risk assessment
* Identification of critical information assets

## PROTECT

Relevant controls:

* Multi-Factor Authentication
* Access control
* Encryption
* Security awareness training
* Endpoint protection
* Backup protection

## DETECT

Relevant controls:

* Security logging
* Monitoring
* Anomaly detection
* Account activity monitoring

## RESPOND

Relevant activities:

* Incident response plan
* Incident analysis
* Containment
* Communication
* Escalation procedures

## RECOVER

Relevant activities:

* Backup restoration
* Recovery procedures
* Business continuity
* Lessons learned
* Security improvement

## Conclusion

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework provides a structured approach for identifying, protecting against, detecting, responding to and recovering from cybersecurity risks.

The framework was used in this project to organize the assessment and connect identified risks with appropriate security controls.
