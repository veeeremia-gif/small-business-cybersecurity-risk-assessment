# Company Profile

## 1. Company Overview

**Company:** NordSecure Consulting GmbH
**Industry:** Business Consulting
**Location:** Hamburg, Germany
**Employees:** 25
**Business Model:** B2B consulting services

NordSecure Consulting GmbH is a fictional small consulting company providing professional services to business customers.

The company relies on digital systems for customer communication, document management, project management, invoicing and internal collaboration.

## 2. IT Environment

* Company laptops
* Business email
* Customer database
* Company website
* Internal collaboration tools

The company's critical business activities include:

The company processes and stores:

## 5. Assessment Objective

The objective of this project is to identify cybersecurity risks affecting the organization and develop practical recommendations to reduce those risks.

The assessment focuses on:

* Asset identification
* Threat identification
* Risk assessment
* Security controls
* Cybersecurity governance
* Risk mitigation

The assessment is based on the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0.
